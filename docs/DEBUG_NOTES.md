## Fixes Made

### Interaction Ray

Problem:

- The ray needed to follow the first-person camera direction, but directly copying the full global transform onto a child ray can create awkward transform behavior.

Fix:

- `scripts/player.gd` now copies the camera global position and global rotation onto `InteractRay`, then calls `force_raycast_update()`.

Why:

- The player interacts with what the camera is actually looking at.
- RayCast3D still stays inside the player scene hierarchy.

### Stand-Up Direction

Problem:

- Standing up from the gaming chair could move the player toward the desk instead of away from it.

Fix:

- `scripts/player.gd` now moves the player backward from the sitting orientation when standing.

Why:

- It prevents the player from clipping into the desk or monitors after leaving the chair.

### Collision and Interactable Ownership

Problem:

- Helper `StaticBody3D` nodes were being added to the `interactable` group even though they do not implement `interact()` or `interact_hint`.

Fix:

- Collision bodies now store `interactable_owner` metadata but are not added to the public `interactable` group.

Why:

- The ray can still resolve the real object owner.
- The project keeps the interactable interface clean: only actual interactable object scripts join the group.

### Player Collision Layers

Problem:

- The player collision layer and mask were implicit.

Fix:

- `scripts/main.gd` explicitly sets the player collision layer and mask.

Why:

- This makes collision behavior easier to reason about and keeps the project aligned with the planned world/interactable layer split.

## Known Limitations

- The current room uses primitive meshes generated by `scripts/main.gd`; imported `.glb` models can replace these later.
- Audio players are supported by scripts, but actual `.ogg` files are not yet included.
- Monitor UI is a working placeholder desktop, not a full fake OS yet.
- Some standalone furniture scene files are minimal placeholders; the complete v1 playable scene is `scenes/main.tscn`.
