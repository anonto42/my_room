# Game story & concept
## Title idea: "My Room" or "Night Shift"
You play as a guy who lives alone in an apartment. The world outside doesn't matter — your world is your room. You wake up, you turn things on, you sit at your multi-monitor computer setup and lose track of time. The vibe is cozy, realistic, and immersive — like a life simulator focused on one small space.
Version 1 loop: Player walks into the room → turns on fan/AC → sits at the gaming chair → turns on the PC → hands rest on keyboard and mouse → screens come to life.
