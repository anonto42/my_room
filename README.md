# Game story and concept

## Title idea: "New Harbor Nights"

You start in a small safehouse room with a bed, PC setup, lights, fan, AC, and a gate. The room is still the personal hub, but the real game now begins when the player opens the gate into New Harbor City.

Basic story:

- The city has started waking back up after a long quiet period.
- The player is the first local systems keeper: not a superhero, just the person who can connect devices, inspect city command points, and understand what each district needs.
- The apartment is the base. The city is the project.
- Each district will later grow into shops, homes, transit, jobs, social stories, and 3D asset-rich spaces.

Current loop:

- Wake in the safehouse.
- Turn on room systems if needed.
- Open the gate.
- Explore the city block.
- Inspect map, power, market, transit, and water points.
- Use what is learned to unlock future city upgrades.
